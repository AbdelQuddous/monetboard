For AdMob publishers

Know where your revenue stands, and why it moved.

MonetBoard reads your AdMob report and tells you what you earned, how it compares with the previous period, and what caused the change: traffic or pricing, which app, which country, which ad format.

Get it on Google Play Free, with an optional one-time upgrade to remove ads.

Answers, not just numbers.

Every explanation is calculated on your device from your own AdMob data, and shows the figures behind it. Nothing is guessed or generated.

At a glance

Estimated earnings, the change against an equal-length window, and a daily trend with unusual days called out.

Signals that explain

Volume versus yield, biggest movers, delivery shifts and concentration risk, ranked by impact.

Investigate any change

Break results down by app, country, ad format or ad unit, and follow the funnel from requests to clicks.

Private and read-only.

MonetBoard signs in with Google using read-only AdMob reporting access. It cannot change your ads, payments or account settings.

  • Straight to your device. Reports travel directly from Google to your phone. Your earnings are not stored on our servers.
  • Privacy mode. Hide every amount on screen when you share your screen or work in public.
  • Your call. Revoke access at any time from your Google account.
  • Try first. Demo mode opens a realistic sample portfolio before you connect an account.
